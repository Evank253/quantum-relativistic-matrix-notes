\section{GR, negative energy, wormholes}

In GR, $T_{\mu\nu}$ sources curvature. Ordinary matter satisfies the null energy condition (NEC)
\[
T_{\mu\nu}k^\mu k^\nu\ge 0
\qquad\text{for all null }k^\mu.
\]
A traversable wormhole throat that stays open requires a NEC violation on some null congruence through the throat. For the Morris--Thorne shape function $b(r)$,
\[
\rho(r)=-\frac{c^4}{8\pi G}\frac{b'(r)}{r^2},
\]
and the combination $\rho+\tau$ at the throat is negative if the throat is to flare out.

That is a constraint, not a construction. Casimir energy in a static cavity is a real, small NEC-violating effect; it does not give a macroscopic passenger throat. Chronology protection (Hawking) is the conjecture that quantum backreaction destroys time machines; it is not a theorem in full semiclassical gravity.

Alcubierre warp metrics also require exotic stress-energy. Positive-energy matter only focuses geodesics (Raychaudhuri).

Holographic wormholes that exist in the literature (Gao--Jafferis--Wall, later island constructions) inject negative null energy by a \emph{boundary} double-trace coupling, not by bulk exotic matter sitting in a Morris--Thorne chart. Islands compute radiation entropy. They do not replace $T_{UU}<0$ at a passenger throat.

\section{JT gravity, islands, Page curve}

Jackiw--Teitelboim dilaton gravity in AdS$_2$, coupled to matter. Generalized entropy of a region $R$:
\[
S(R)=\min_{\mathrm{ext}\,I}\Bigl(\frac{\mathrm{Area}(\partial I)}{4G_N}+S_{\mathrm{semi-cl}}(R\cup I)\Bigr).
\]
In JT the ``area'' is the dilaton value $\Phi(\partial I)/4G_N$.

\subsection{Page time (eternal + radiation toy)}
Radiation entropy growing at rate $\Gamma=\pi c/(3\beta)$, plus an optional evaporation term $\alpha$. Competition
\[
S_{\mathrm{rad}}(t)=\min\bigl\{\Gamma t,\; S_i-\alpha t\bigr\}
\quad\Rightarrow\quad
t_{\mathrm{Page}}=\frac{S_i}{\Gamma+\alpha}.
\]
Check used in-session: $S_i=100$, $c=1$, $\beta=2\pi$, $\alpha=0$ gives $\Gamma=1/6$ and $t_{\mathrm{Page}}=600$. Scrambling time $t_*\sim(\beta/2\pi)\log S_i$ is parametrically shorter.

Replica wormholes: the connected replica saddle dominates after the Page time and implements the swap empty island $\to$ interior island. Information in $R$ is encoded in correlations, not as a macroscopic worldline through $I$.

\section{Disk, trumpet, cylinder, SFF}

Poincar\'e / Schwarzian conventions used in the lab code:
\[
Z_{\mathrm{disk}}(\beta)=\Bigl(\frac{\pi}{\beta}\Bigr)^{3/2}\exp\Bigl(S_0+\frac{2\pi^2 C}{\beta}\Bigr),
\]
\[
Z_{\mathrm{tr}}(\beta,b)=\frac{e^{-b^2/4\beta}}{\sqrt{4\pi\beta}}.
\]
Double trumpet (cylinder):
\[
Z_{0,2}(\beta_1,\beta_2)
=\int_0^\infty b\,db\;Z_{\mathrm{tr}}(\beta_1,b)\,Z_{\mathrm{tr}}(\beta_2,b)
=\frac{\sqrt{\beta_1\beta_2}}{2\pi(\beta_1+\beta_2)}.
\]
Numeric quadrature at $\beta_1=\beta_2=2$ matched the closed form to relative error $\sim 10^{-16}$.

Continue $\beta\pm it$:
\[
\bigl|Z_{0,2}(\beta+it,\beta-it)\bigr|
=\frac{\sqrt{\beta^2+t^2}}{4\pi\beta}
\;\xrightarrow{t\gg\beta}\;
\frac{t}{4\pi\beta}.
\]
That linear growth is the SFF \emph{ramp} from topology $(g,n)=(0,2)$, not from eigenvalue rigidity put in by hand. At $t=20$, $\beta=2$: exact $0.7997$, linear asymptote $0.7958$.

Disconnected Schwarzian SFF (two disks):
\[
\mathrm{SFF}_{\mathrm{disc}}(t)
\propto (\beta^2+t^2)^{-3/2}
\exp\Bigl(\frac{4\pi^2 C\beta}{\beta^2+t^2}\Bigr)
\;\xrightarrow{t\gg\beta}\; t^{-3}.
\]
GUE connected form factor: ramp $t/t_H$ until Heisenberg time $t_H\sim e^{S}$, then plateau $1$.

\section{SYK, chords, Lyapunov}

\subsection{Moments from the $q$-oscillator}
Double-scaled SYK chords: crossing weight $q=e^{-\lambda}$. Transfer matrix
\[
[n]_q=\frac{1-q^n}{1-q},
\qquad
H|n\rangle=\sqrt{[n]_q}\,|n-1\rangle+\sqrt{[n+1]_q}\,|n+1\rangle,
\]
\[
m_n(q)=\langle 0|H^n|0\rangle.
\]
Limits checked in code: $q=0$ gives Catalan numbers; $q=1$ gives double factorials $(2n-1)!!$. $q\to 1^-$ and low energy recovers the Schwarzian.

\subsection{Lyapunov}

\section{JT as a matrix integral (SSS)}
\[
